# Phase 5 — Readout and Retest

**Goal**: close the loop.

---

## Readouts

Two readouts are delivered:

- **Technical** — with the client's engineering team. Walks through each finding, the reproduction, and the remediation.
- **Executive** — with leadership. Covers business impact, regulatory exposure, and the residual risk after remediation.

The two readouts use the same evidence, presented to different audiences. The technical readout is not a subset of the executive; it is a different conversation.

## Retest

After remediation, the client can request a retest. Retest runs the same cases that raised findings, verifies each closure, and produces a retest addendum.

The retest addendum states:

- What was retested
- What closed
- What did not close, and why
- Any new findings raised during retest

Retest is included from single-system level up, within 30 days of the client's remediation notice.

## Closure

A finding is closed when the harness that asserts the property passes, not when the client says it is fixed. The harness is delivered with the remediation guidance, so the client can run it themselves.

## Post-engagement

After the retest, the engagement is closed. The evidence pack is delivered. The 30-day artefact retention begins.

If the client requests ongoing assurance, the engagement moves to a subscription model. Release retests and periodic re-runs keep the evidence current between sales cycles.

## Exit condition

Retest addendum delivered, or 30 days elapsed since remediation notice, whichever comes first.
